Background: Like other campus information systems, Campus Space Information (CSI) requires a list of UW organizational units that can be applied for various business purposes.

The Assigned Org field is used to track which UW organizational unit is assigned to each room.

  • The Assigned Org is formally assigned and effectively responsible for the room.
  • Each room will have only one Assigned Org.
  • We also track which specific UW organizational units occupy rooms using the Associated Org child records.
  • A room can have multiple Associated Orgs.

Cost Center Hierarchies will be utilized for space assignments.

Cost Center Hierarchy Levels in CSI
  • Only CCHL7s are applied to space.
  • CSI is importing a list of all CCHL records (CCHL1 through CCHL8) from Workday. Uses of multiple levels include:
    • Allows us to aggregate and report space data at all levels of the hierarchy from CCHL1 through CCHL7 within CSI.
    • CCHL8 records will be retained in the system for potential future application to space.
