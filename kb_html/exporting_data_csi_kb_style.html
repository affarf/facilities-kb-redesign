There are user-based reasons for using facility or room details outside of the CSI environment. This may be as simple as printing a report or exporting a table of results to manipulate data offline.

There are two types of exporting in CSI software: Reports and Floor Plans.

Back to CSI Lesson List
From Reports

Reports are the only way to export tabular data from CSI. Report exports can be printed or used to work with tabular data offline.

  • To prepare & export data – See the Customizing Reports lesson for detailed help to prepare and export space data.
From Floor Plans

Floor plans can be very helpful to download for many uses.

  • To prepare – See the Viewing a Floor Plan lesson to select the desired floor plan and details for downloading.
  • To export – See the Printing a Floor Plan lesson to optimize PDF exports.
User Responsibilities

Reports are exported with a report name, date, and disclaimer information. For more details, see the Access to CSI lesson.

Important: Be mindful of storing and sharing any floor plans with sensitive areas.
